Baler and Mount Samat are farther away; each works well as a separate trip.

Travel tips

  • The dry season (roughly November to April) is best for hiking and camping.
  • Book a licensed guide and 4x4 for Mount Pinatubo; access is arranged locally.
  • Bring cash; many small towns and trailheads do not accept cards.
  • Surf season in Baler usually runs from around September to February.
  • Start treks early to avoid the midday heat.
