Activities & Experiences

Adventure

  • Trek to the Mount Pinatubo crater lake by 4x4 and on foot
  • Camp overnight at Anawangin Cove
  • Learn to surf at Sabang Beach in Baler
  • Hike the trails and caves of Biak-na-Bato National Park

Culture

  • Tour Barasoain Church and the Malolos heritage sites
  • Visit the Mount Samat National Shrine and its museum
  • See the lantern makers of San Fernando at work
  • Learn about Aeta culture in the Zambales and Tarlac highlands

Food

  • Eat sisig at its birthplace in Angeles, Pampanga
  • Try tibok-tibok, a soft carabao-milk dessert from Pampanga
  • Buy pastillas de leche in San Miguel, Bulacan
  • Sample fresh rice dishes from Nueva Ecija, the rice granary of the Philippines
