Visit Central Luzon

Explore volcano trails, heritage towns, and the best food in the Philippines.

Welcome to Region III

Central Luzon is made up of seven provinces: Aurora, Bataan, Bulacan, Nueva Ecija, Pampanga, Tarlac, and Zambales. It is home to the vast Central Plain, the crater lake of Mount Pinatubo, quiet coves on the Zambales coast, surf towns in Aurora, and historic churches and shrines tied to the story of the Philippine nation. It is also known for Kapampangan cooking, and it is close enough to Manila for an easy weekend trip.
